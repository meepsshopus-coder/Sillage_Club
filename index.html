Good to know

Questions, answered.

Where are the fragrances sourced?

Our current fragrances are imported from France to our warehouse before being prepared for delivery. We work through fragrance supply channels that allow us to source branded products at prices below traditional retail in selected cases.

Why are the prices lower than typical retail?

Sillage Club is built around a curated inventory model and direct sourcing. Lower operating and sourcing costs can allow us to offer selected fragrances at a more accessible price.

Can I return a fragrance?

Return requests must be made within 14 days of delivery. Return shipping is paid by the customer. Unopened, undamaged items that simply no longer suit your plans may be eligible for store credit after inspection. Please contact us before sending anything back.

How long does shipping take?

Approximately 3–7 business days within the U.S., 1–4 business days for local Arizona delivery, and 10–15 business days internationally. Carrier and destination can affect actual delivery time.
